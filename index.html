Inside a PC

A personal computer is a handful of parts that each do one job. This page lists the main ones.

The main parts

CPU

The processor runs program instructions. More cores and higher clock speeds mean it can do more work at once.

RAM

Memory holds the data your programs are using right now. It is fast but empties when the PC is turned off.

Storage

An SSD or hard drive keeps your files and operating system permanently. SSDs are much faster than hard drives.

GPU

The graphics card draws the image on your screen. It matters most for games, video editing and 3D work.

Motherboard and power supply

The motherboard connects every part together, and the power supply gives each one the electricity it needs.
